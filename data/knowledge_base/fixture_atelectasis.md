# Atelectasis — development note

Atelectasis is the collapse or incomplete expansion of part or all of
a lung. On a chest radiograph it can appear as increased opacity,
volume loss, or displacement of fissures.

Reports commonly describe atelectasis as "linear", "plate-like",
"basilar", "lobar", or "subsegmental". It is often described alongside
consolidation, since the two can be difficult to distinguish on
radiographs.

This fixture is used to test the RADIANT evidence retrieval pipeline.
It is not clinical guidance.
