# Cardiomegaly — development note

Cardiomegaly is an enlargement of the cardiac silhouette. On a frontal
chest radiograph it is typically described in relation to the thoracic
width — for example, a cardiothoracic ratio above 0.5 is often used as
a threshold, though the exact value varies by technique and patient.

Reports commonly describe cardiomegaly as "mild", "moderate", or
"severe", or as "enlarged cardiac silhouette". Normal reports often
state "cardiac silhouette is normal in size" or "no cardiomegaly".

Cardiomegaly frequently appears in RADIANT demo runs because the
torchxrayvision NIH checkpoint often scores it at the top of its
outputs. This makes it a useful fixture for testing the pipeline.

This fixture is used to test the RADIANT evidence retrieval pipeline.
It is not clinical guidance.
