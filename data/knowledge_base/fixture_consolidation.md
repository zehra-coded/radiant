# Consolidation — development note

Consolidation refers to replacement of air in the lung parenchyma by
fluid, cells, or other material. On a chest radiograph it appears as
an area of increased opacity that may contain air bronchograms.

Reports commonly describe consolidation with a location ("right lower
lobe consolidation", "left basilar consolidation") and often hedge
with phrases like "possibly representing consolidation" or "cannot
exclude consolidation".

Consolidation is one of RADIANT's critical labels. A conflict on
consolidation between image and report forces overall_consistency =
conflicting.

This fixture is used to test the RADIANT evidence retrieval pipeline.
It is not clinical guidance.
