# Pleural effusion — development note

Pleural effusion is the accumulation of fluid in the pleural space. On
a frontal chest radiograph it can manifest as blunting of the
costophrenic angle, a meniscus sign, or opacification of the lower
hemithorax depending on volume and patient position.

Radiology reports commonly describe pleural effusion as "small",
"moderate", or "large", with a laterality (left, right, bilateral).
Report phrasing often uses "no pleural effusion" or "no significant
pleural fluid".

Effusion is one of the critical labels in RADIANT's fusion layer. A
disagreement between the image model and the report on effusion forces
overall_consistency = conflicting.

This fixture is used to test the RADIANT evidence retrieval pipeline.
It is not clinical guidance.
