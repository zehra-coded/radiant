# RADIANT — Demo video script (5 minutes)

A scripted walkthrough for recording the hackathon submission video.
Target runtime: **4 minutes 30 seconds to 5 minutes.**

Record at 1920×1080 (or 1280×720), screen + microphone. Do a dry run
without recording first.

---

## Setup — before you start recording

Two PowerShell windows must be running:

**Window 1 (API):**
```
cd C:\Users\Anyone\radiant-workspace\radiant
F:\radiant-venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
F:\radiant-venv\Scripts\python.exe -m uvicorn backend.api.main:app --port 8000
```

**Window 2 (Streamlit):**
```
cd C:\Users\Anyone\radiant-workspace\radiant
F:\radiant-venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
streamlit run frontend\streamlit_app.py
```

Browser open at `http://localhost:8501`.

**Image to upload:** `D:\NIH-ChestXray14\images\00000001_000.png`
**Report to paste:** contents of `data\synthetic\report_conflict.txt`

---

## Segment 1 — Title and disclaimer (0:00 – 0:20)

**Show:** the Streamlit header with the disclaimer.

**Say:**
> "This is RADIANT — a multimodal chest X-ray triage and evidence
> assistant. Before anything else: RADIANT is a research prototype.
> It does not diagnose, it does not recommend treatment, and every
> output requires review by a licensed physician. That disclaimer is
> always visible. RADIANT never hides what it is."

---

## Segment 2 — The problem (0:20 – 0:40)

**Show:** stay on the header.

**Say:**
> "Chest X-ray AI models produce ranked finding scores. In clinical
> use, those scores are read alongside the radiologist's report — and
> the two signals sometimes disagree. A model that says effusion
> present on an image where the report says no pleural effusion is
> exactly what a reviewer needs to see. Today, no tool surfaces that
> disagreement systematically. RADIANT does."

---

## Segment 3 — Upload and paste (0:40 – 1:00)

**Show:** upload `00000001_000.png`, paste the conflict report.

**Say:**
> "We upload a real NIH ChestX-ray14 image — ground truth labeled
> Cardiomegaly. And we paste a synthetic report that explicitly
> negates both pneumothorax and pleural effusion. Watch what happens
> when the image model sees those findings."

---

## Segment 4 — Analyze and progress (1:00 – 1:30)

**Show:** click Analyze. Watch the seven progress steps tick off.

**Say:**
> "Each step appears only after the backend confirms it completed.
> There is no fake progress. The frontend consumes a Server-Sent
> Events stream from the FastAPI backend. Vision runs DenseNet-121
> on CPU. NLP extracts findings with a deterministic regex extractor.
> The cross-modal verifier compares them. And here — priority is
> URGENT_REVIEW, and human review is required."

---

## Segment 5 — The differentiator (1:30 – 2:15)

**Show:** click the Consistency tab. Point at the two conflicts.

**Say:**
> "This is the differentiator. The image model says Effusion and
> Pneumothorax are present. The report says they're negated. RADIANT
> flags both as conflicts. It does not pick a winner. It does not
> claim the model is right or the report is wrong. It escalates the
> disagreement to a human reviewer. That's what makes this different
> from a classifier."

---

## Segment 6 — Traceability (2:15 – 2:40)

**Show:** click the Uncertainty tab.

**Say:**
> "Every reason the priority is URGENT_REVIEW is listed here, each
> one traceable to a specific signal. Two vision/report conflicts.
> Three critical findings present. And ten low-confidence findings —
> the vision model clustered many scores near 0.5. We don't hide
> that. The uncertainty layer flags it. The reviewer sees exactly
> why this case is being escalated."

---

## Segment 7 — Evidence (2:40 – 3:05)

**Show:** click the Evidence tab. Expand one chunk.

**Say:**
> "Evidence is retrieved from a FAISS index. Every chunk carries its
> source, license, and provenance. These are labeled as development
> fixtures — 'not authoritative clinical guidance' — because that's
> what they are. That changes when real licensed sources are added."

---

## Segment 8 — Explanation and the LLM (3:05 – 3:50)

**Show:** click the Explanation tab. Scroll to the LLM Backend panel.

**Say:**
> "The explanation is generated by DeepSeek-V4-Flash, hosted on AMD
> GPUs and served via the AMD Token Factory API. It cites real
> chunks. It does not diagnose. It does not recommend treatment.
> It never sees the image. During our evaluation, our network
> dropped mid-run. The LLM became unreachable. RADIANT fell back to
> the deterministic template reporter and produced the same
> structure, the same citations, the same disclaimer — without the
> LLM. The LLM is optional. The safety architecture is not."

---

## Segment 9 — Safety gate (3:50 – 4:15)

**Show:** click the Safety tab.

**Say:**
> "The safety gate runs twice: once before generation, once after.
> The post-check validates every citation against the retrieved set,
> scans for diagnosis and treatment language, and blocks the output
> if anything is wrong. During evaluation, three of five LLM attempts
> were rejected by this gate and replaced by the template. Zero
> fabricated citations reached the output. This is what fail-closed
> looks like in practice."

---

## Segment 10 — Numbers (4:15 – 4:45)

**Show:** switch to a browser tab with `docs/evaluation.md` on GitHub.

**Say:**
> "Every number in our evaluation comes from a real execution on
> real data. 1000 real NIH test images for vision. 2000 real
> Rad-SpRL reports for NLP. Six evaluation components. 105 passing
> tests. The numbers aren't always flattering — our NLP hedge
> agreement is 0.11, our anatomy agreement is 0.23, and we document
> those as weaknesses. Anything we couldn't measure is marked
> 'NOT RUN' with a reason. Nothing in this repository is invented."

---

## Segment 11 — Closing (4:45 – 5:00)

**Show:** the GitHub repo landing page.

**Say:**
> "The full source, tests, docs, and evaluation are public at
> github.com/zehra-coded/radiant. MIT licensed. Every commit is
> traceable. RADIANT is a research prototype, not a medical device.
> Thank you."

## After recording

- Trim dead air at start and end
- Verify audio is audible throughout
- Export as MP4, H.264, 1080p or 720p
- Target file size under 200 MB
- Filename: `radiant-demo-5min.mp4`

## If something breaks mid-recording

**Stop, fix, restart from the last natural break.** Common issues:

- API not responding → restart Window 1
- Streamlit "Could not reach API" → check port matches
- LLM call fails → fallback engages automatically; keep recording,
  point at the fallback as a feature
